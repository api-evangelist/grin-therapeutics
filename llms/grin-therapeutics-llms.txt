# GRIN Therapeutics

> GRIN Therapeutics, Inc. is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing precision therapeutics
> for pediatric neurodevelopmental disorders caused by NMDA receptor dysfunction. It is an affiliate
> of Neurvati Neurosciences, a Blackstone Life Sciences portfolio company. Its lead investigational
> asset is radiprodil, an orally bioavailable selective negative allosteric modulator of the GluN2B
> subunit of the NMDA receptor, in development for GRIN-related neurodevelopmental disorder
> (GRIN-NDD), tuberous sclerosis complex (TSC) and focal cortical dysplasia (FCD) type II.

## Science and pipeline

- Lead asset: radiprodil — orally bioavailable, selective negative allosteric modulator of the NMDA receptor GluN2B subunit.
- Indications: GRIN-NDD (gain-of-function variants in GRIN1, GRIN2A, GRIN2B, GRIN2D), tuberous sclerosis complex, focal cortical dysplasia type II.
- Honeycomb — Phase 1b/2a in GRIN-NDD (gain-of-function). Complete; topline data Q3 2024.
- Beeline — global Phase 3 registrational trial in GRIN-NDD (gain-of-function). Enrolling in the US, UK and Europe.
- Astroscape — Phase 1b/2a in TSC and FCD type II. Enrolling.
- GRIN-NDD Natural History Study — observational. Enrolling.
- Designations: FDA Breakthrough Therapy, FDA Orphan Drug, FDA Rare Pediatric Disease, EMA PRIME, EMA Orphan Drug.
- Partnership: exclusive collaboration with Angelini Pharma (May 2025) to develop and commercialise radiprodil outside North America.
